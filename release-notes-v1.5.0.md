# solar-layout-card v1.5.0

## Added

- Editor: next to "Attach to panel…", an inverter/micro-inverter row now has a
  "Show in picture" checkbox. Turn it on and the inverter is nested as a small
  badge in the corner of its panel (name and reading shown on hover) instead of
  a separate tile next to it. Any connection lines to/from that inverter are
  removed when you enable it, since it's now part of the panel rather than a
  free-standing, wireable node.

## Updating

Update via HACS and then do a hard browser refresh (or clear the `.gz` in
`www/community/solar-layout-card/`) if you don't see the change immediately.
